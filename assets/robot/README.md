# house_robot assets

`house_robot.urdf` is generated by `tools/gen_robot.py` (dimensions in `tools/params.py`); do not edit by hand.

## Layout (robot frame: +x forward, +y left, z up)
- Virtual holonomic base: `world_anchor` -> prismatic `base_x_joint` -> prismatic `base_y_joint` -> revolute `base_yaw_joint` -> `base_link` (0.45 x 0.45 platform, top at z=0.25). The four mecanum wheels are visual only.
- Lift: prismatic `lift_joint` (0 to 0.5 m) on a column at the rear-left; carriage carries the PiPER base.
- Arm: AgileX PiPER (`joint1`..`joint6`, gripper `joint7`/`joint8`), base at (-0.07, +0.17) on the carriage, facing forward.
- Basket: open-top box fixed on the platform, inner 0.35 (x) x 0.30 (y) x 0.20 deep, centre (0.04, -0.05).

Why this placement: driving +y in the corridor puts the washer on the robot's left, so the arm sits on the left half (+y) of the platform, 0.17 m closer to the washer. The basket is on the opposite (right) half but in front of the arm base, so basket and washer opening are at about -63 deg and +90 deg from the arm's forward axis, both inside the +-150 deg range of `joint1`. The lift raises the shoulder to reach the 0.95 m opening and lowers it to reach into the basket. A Genesis IK check (position only, `link6`) found both targets reachable.
